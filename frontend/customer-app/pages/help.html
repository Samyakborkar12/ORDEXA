Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using ORDEXA Smart Queue

How do I join a queue and get a token? ▼
You can either tap Explore to find your clinic, bank or hospital, or tap Scan QR to scan the standee at the entrance. Select your required service and confirm to get an instant digital token.
Can I leave the waiting area while in queue? ▼
Yes! That is the core benefit of ORDEXA. You can go to a café, run an errand, or wait comfortably. Keep the Live Queue tab open on your phone; it will beep and notify you when 2 people are ahead of you.
What if I am running late? ▼
Tap Hold Spot in the queue view to pause your token for 5 to 15 minutes. The counter will serve the next person while holding your position. Tap Rejoin as soon as you're back.
How is the estimated wait time calculated? ▼
Wait times are calculated based on the number of customers currently ahead of you multiplied by the average service handling duration of the assigned counter desk.

Still need assistance?

Our support desk is available to assist you with active queue queries.
